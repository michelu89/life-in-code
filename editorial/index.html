II began my journey as an apprentice in 2009, writing my first lines of code and learning the foundations of software development. Over the years, I've evolved from student to engineer to technical lead, working across the full stack with a focus on semantic technologies and AI-powered systems.

As Technical Lead at Bosch Connected Industry, I architect semantic data models using ontologies and knowledge graphs that enable AI systems to understand context and relationships. I lead teams across frontend, backend, and semantic data modeling—from requirements analysis through implementation. As a main contributor to Eclipse ESMF (Semantic Modeling Framework), I develop the Aspect Model Editor used for standardized semantic modeling in industrial IoT. I also specialize in security and compliance, building automated workflows for CVE remediation and license analysis.
